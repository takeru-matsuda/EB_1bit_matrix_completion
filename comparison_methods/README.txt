This repository includes MATLAB codes for comparison methods (MMGN, TraceNorm, and MaxNorm) of 1-bit matrix completion. They are based on the codes originally provided by Xiaoqian Liu at https://github.com/Xiaoqian-Liu/MMGN. These codes are distributed under the MIT License. We sincerely thank the authors for making their code publicly available. 

- MaxNorm was modified to work even when M = [], because M is not available in practice.

- TraceNorm uses One-Bit Matrix Completion (1BMC) Toolbox by Mark A. Davenport at https://mdav.ece.gatech.edu/software/. The toolbox is distributed under the GNU General Public License (GPL) v3.
